## Question — Analyze survey results and persona

**Lab-book question:** SET C: Analysis.

### Solution

Analyze collected survey responses and convert the findings into actionable UX requirements.

### Analysis format

| Research observation | UX implication |
|---|---|
| Users want quick search | Keep search prominent |
| Users compare ratings | Show ratings on restaurant cards |
| Users want delivery certainty | Display estimated delivery time |
| Users dislike complicated checkout | Reduce unnecessary checkout steps |
| Users use mobile devices | Design for touch interaction |
| Users need clear confirmation | Provide success and error feedback |

### Step-by-step analysis

1. Export or inspect the Google Forms response summary.
2. Group similar responses.
3. Identify the most frequently mentioned needs.
4. Identify common frustrations.
5. Identify behavioral patterns.
6. Convert the findings into design requirements.
7. Compare the requirements with the persona.
8. Prioritize requirements for the first design iteration.

### Example design requirements

- Search must be available from the home screen.
- Restaurant cards should show name, rating, price information and estimated delivery.
- Checkout should use a simple linear flow.
- Confirmation should clearly indicate successful order placement.
- Text and controls should remain readable on a mobile screen.

### Result

Survey findings were analyzed and converted into actionable UX requirements.

### Conclusion

Research analysis connects raw user responses to concrete design decisions.
