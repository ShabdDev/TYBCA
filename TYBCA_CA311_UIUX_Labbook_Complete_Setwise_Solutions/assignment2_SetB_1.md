## Question — Create a persona

**Lab-book question:** SET B: Persona.

### Solution

Create a fictional but realistic persona based on survey findings.

### Persona

**Name:** Priya Kulkarni  
**Age:** 24  
**Occupation:** Software trainee  
**Location:** Pune  
**Technology familiarity:** High  
**Device:** Android smartphone

### Goals

- Order food quickly.
- Find restaurants near her location.
- Compare ratings and prices.
- Track delivery status.
- Complete payment with minimum steps.

### Frustrations / Pain Points

- Too many screens before checkout.
- Unclear delivery time.
- Difficulty finding suitable restaurants.
- Unexpected charges appearing late in checkout.
- Small or unclear controls.

### Behaviors

- Uses food delivery apps several times a week.
- Checks ratings before ordering.
- Uses search and filters.
- Prefers digital payment.
- Frequently uses the mobile application rather than a desktop website.

### Needs

- Simple navigation.
- Clear restaurant information.
- Visible delivery estimate.
- Transparent pricing.
- Easy checkout.
- Clear confirmation after payment.

### Design implications

The persona suggests designing:

1. A prominent search bar.
2. Useful filters.
3. Clear restaurant cards.
4. Visible ratings and delivery estimates.
5. A simple checkout flow.
6. Large, readable touch targets.
7. Clear success/error feedback.

### Result

A research-based persona was created.

### Conclusion

A persona converts research observations into a relatable target-user profile that can guide design decisions.
