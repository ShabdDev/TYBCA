## Question — Create a digital wireframe

**Lab-book question:** SET B: Digital wireframe.

### Solution

Convert the paper wireframe into a digital low-fidelity wireframe using Figma.

### Procedure

1. Open Figma.
2. Create a mobile frame.
3. Recreate the paper wireframe using rectangles, lines and text.
4. Keep the design primarily grayscale.
5. Use placeholder boxes for images.
6. Add labels such as Search, Categories, Restaurant, Orders and Profile.
7. Align components consistently.
8. Maintain spacing between sections.
9. Review the layout at mobile size.

### Digital wireframe components

- Header
- Location
- Profile
- Search field
- Category cards
- Restaurant cards
- Bottom navigation

### Important rule

The purpose is structure, not final visual styling. Therefore avoid spending time on detailed colors, imagery, shadows and decorative effects.

### Result

A digital low-fidelity wireframe was created in Figma.

### Conclusion

The paper concept was successfully converted into a structured digital wireframe.
