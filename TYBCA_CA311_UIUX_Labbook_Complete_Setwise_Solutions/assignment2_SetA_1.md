## Question — Conduct a survey

**Lab-book question:** SET A: Survey.

### Solution

Create a Google Form to collect information about users' requirements before designing an application.

### Survey topic

**Topic:** User requirements for a food delivery application.

### Suggested questions

1. What is your age group?
   - Below 18
   - 18–25
   - 26–35
   - 36–50
   - Above 50
2. How often do you order food online?
   - Daily
   - Several times a week
   - Weekly
   - Rarely
3. Which feature is most important?
   - Fast delivery
   - Easy search
   - Offers
   - Restaurant ratings
   - Easy payment
4. What problem do you face most often?
   - Difficult navigation
   - Too many choices
   - Delivery uncertainty
   - Payment problems
   - Other
5. Do you prefer tracking your order in real time?
   - Yes
   - No
6. Which payment method do you usually use?
7. What would make the app easier to use?
8. How important are clear food images and descriptions?
   - Very important
   - Important
   - Neutral
   - Less important
   - Not important

### Procedure

1. Open Google Forms.
2. Create a blank form.
3. Enter the title and description.
4. Add the questions.
5. Mark required questions as required.
6. Share the form with participants.
7. Collect responses.
8. Review the response summary.
9. Identify repeated needs, behaviors and pain points.

### Sample analysis

Assume the collected responses indicate that users frequently request:

- Simple search and filtering.
- Clear delivery status.
- Easy checkout.
- Restaurant ratings.
- Fewer unnecessary steps.

These findings can be used to create a persona and guide the later interface design.

### Result

A user survey was created and responses were analyzed to identify user needs and pain points.

### Conclusion

User research provides evidence for design decisions instead of relying only on assumptions.
